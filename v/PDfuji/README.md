# PROGYNON / DEPOT — A Molecular Dossier

An interactive research dossier on **プロギノン・デポー筋注10mg** (Fuji Pharma, estradiol valerate 10 mg/mL), its official Japanese label, MtF GAHT guidelines, pharmacokinetics, safety, insurance and the 2026 supply fracture.

- **Visual site:** `index.html`
- **Full dossier:** `dossier.html`
- **Raw Markdown:** `dossier/*.md`
- **Data cut-off:** 2026-11-26
- **Not medical advice.** The product is a Japanese prescription drug; MtF GAHT use is off-label and, in principle, uninsured.

Built as a static site. No frameworks, no tracking, no external JS. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts; everything else is local.
