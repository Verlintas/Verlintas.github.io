4. 効能又は効果OFFICIAL / JP

無月経、月経周期異常（稀発月経、多発月経）、月経量異常（過少月経、過多月経）、月経困難症、機能性子宮出血、子宮発育不全症、卵巣欠落症状、更年期障害、不妊症

Amenorrhoea · menstrual cycle abnormalities · abnormal menstrual volume · dysmenorrhoea · functional uterine bleeding · uterine hypoplasia · ovarian deficiency symptoms · menopausal disorders · infertility.

FIELD NOTE The list is entirely gynaecological. Neither “gender incongruence” nor “GAHT” appears anywhere in the approved indications.
6. 用法及び用量OFFICIAL / JP

エストラジオール吉草酸エステルとして、通常成人1回5～10mgを1～4週間毎に筋肉内注射する。なお、症状により適宜増減する。

As estradiol valerate, the usual adult dose is 5–10 mg IM once every 1–4 weeks; adjust according to symptoms.

FIELD NOTE This is the gynaecological label dose. Japanese GAHT guidelines list 10 mg/2–3 weeks or 20 mg/2–4 weeks — a different, off-label framework.
2. 禁忌OFFICIAL / JP
  • Estrogen-dependent malignancy (breast, endometrium) or suspicion
  • History of breast cancer
  • Untreated endometrial hyperplasia
  • Thrombophlebitis or pulmonary embolism (current or prior)
  • Arterial thromboembolic disease (CHD, stroke) or prior
  • Severe hepatic impairment
  • Undiagnosed abnormal genital bleeding
  • Pregnancy or possible pregnancy
FIELD NOTE The label’s absolute contraindications are not “relative risks”. For MtF use, the breast-cancer and thrombotic entries still matter; the uterine entries are label-specific.
16. 薬物動態OFFICIAL / FOREIGN DATA

エストラジオール吉草酸エステルは筋肉内投与後、徐々に放出され、エストラジオールと吉草酸に分かれる。血中エストラジオールは投与3～5日後に最高濃度に達し、約4～5日の半減期で低下した（外国人データ）。

After IM injection, the ester is slowly released and split into estradiol and valeric acid. Serum estradiol peaks 3–5 days after injection and declines with an apparent 4–5 day half-life (foreign data).

FIELD NOTE The “half-life” is release-limited (flip-flop), not the true elimination half-life of estradiol. Japanese domestic PK data are absent from the label.

“major” adverse effect.

FOR PATIENTS

Do not self-adjust.

Discuss patches, gel or oral estradiol with your clinician. Do not split ampoules, stockpile grey-market product, or change interval on your own.

FOR CLINICIANS

Plan the bridge.

Review alternatives, monitoring and patient-specific VTE risk. Document the switch. The shortage is not a reason to abandon monitoring.

FOR THE RECORD

One product, one fracture.

Pera-nin’s exit leaves Progynon as virtually the only domestic EV injection. A single-product supply chain is a clinical vulnerability.

Is this product approved for MtF GAHT? +

No. The approved indications are gynaecological. MtF use is off-label in Japan.

What is the Japanese guideline dose? +

Estradiol valerate IM 10 mg every 2–3 weeks, or 20 mg every 2–4 weeks, individualised by serum testosterone and clinical effect.

What do international guidelines say? +

WPATH SOC8 and Endocrine Society 2017: 5–30 mg IM every 2 weeks, or 2–10 mg IM every week; target E2 100–200 pg/mL and T <50 ng/dL. Newer national guidance trends lower.

Is it covered by Japanese insurance? +

GAHT is in principle not covered. A 2026 government advisory opinion records MHLW’s position that no GAHT drug is approved, so it falls outside insurance. Some facilities make individual exceptions after legal sex change.

Why is supply limited? +

Fuji’s June 2026 notice cites orders exceeding expectations; MHLW supply data also lists “raw material procurement”. As of 2026-11-20, release is undetermined.

Can I use it subcutaneously? +

The label is IM only. Subcutaneous use is a community practice, not an approved route; PK and local safety are not formally established.
